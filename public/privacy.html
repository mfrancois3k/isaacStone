Conversations with Wamy

When you start a voice conversation, microphone audio is sent to xAI to understand your questions and generate responses. Typed messages are also processed by xAI. Closing Wamy or ending the conversation stops microphone capture and audio playback.

The conversation transcript is shown in your browser. This interface does not save it across a page refresh. Processing and retention by xAI are governed by its own privacy terms.

Estimate requests

The estimate form and “Prepare estimate email” button open a draft in your email app. The email is sent only when you send it there. If you ask the voice agent to email details or arrange a calendar appointment, it can use the connected services after confirming the action with you.
